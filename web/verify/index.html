What verifying allows

Luger’s Discord application will read your Discord user ID, username and avatar and link them to your Luger account (and your Roblox account, if you came from the app).

It will add you to the Luger Discord server and give you the Verified role.

It may add you to other Discord servers run by Luger, such as a replacement server if the main one is ever lost, or official Luger community servers. It can only do this through servers where Luger’s own bot has been added.

What it never does

Luger never sees your Discord password, email address or messages, and never posts or sends messages as you.

Changing your mind

Remove Luger any time in Discord: User Settings → Authorised Apps → Luger → Deauthorise. Then ask in a ticket and we delete your stored Discord data.

Rules

Verifying means you agree to the full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, including using Luger only in games that allow macros, and being 13 or older (with a parent or guardian’s permission if you are under 18).

Only open this page from Luger or the Luger Discord. Luger staff will never ask for your password or key.
