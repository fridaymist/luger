Refund Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Subscriptions can be cancelled any time. Keys and crystals are delivered instantly, so they usually can’t be refunded once used, but if something is wrong, we’ll put it right.

Cancelling a subscription

Weekly and monthly keys renew automatically. Cancel any time using the link in your Stripe receipt email, or ask us in a Discord ticket. Your key keeps working until the end of the period you’ve paid for, and you won’t be charged again.

When you can get a refund

  • Key never used: if you bought a key and haven’t activated it on any device, ask within 14 days for a full refund.
  • Charged twice or by mistake: we refund duplicate or accidental charges in full.
  • It doesn’t work: if Luger doesn’t work as described on your computer and we can’t fix it, you can have a repair, replacement or refund under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
  • Renewal you forgot about: if a weekly or monthly renewal went through and you haven’t used Luger since, ask within 48 hours and we’ll refund it.

When we can’t refund

Keys that have been activated and crystals or items that have been spent are digital content you asked us to supply straight away, so the 14-day cancellation right no longer applies (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regulation 37). Keys removed for breaking the Terms are not refunded.

How to ask

Open a ticket in the Luger Discord with your receipt email or order number. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 working days.

Chargebacks

Please talk to us first. If a payment is disputed or reversed through your bank, the key or items it paid for are removed automatically.
