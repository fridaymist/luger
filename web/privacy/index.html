Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

We collect as little as we can: your public Roblox details, a scrambled device ID, what you do in Luger so keys and progress work, and Discord details only if you choose to verify. We never sell your data, never read your Roblox login cookie, and never see your card number.

1. Who is responsible

Luger (lugerhub.fun) is the data controller for the information described here. Contact us through the Luger Discord (open a ticket).

The Luger app keeps your settings, macros, recordings and playtime on your own computer. Macros you run locally are not uploaded unless you publish them.

3. What we never do

  • We never read or store your Roblox password or login cookie.
  • We never see or store your full payment card details (Stripe handles those).
  • We never sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help run Luger, under their own privacy and security terms: Supabase (database and servers), Cloudflare (website and security), Stripe (payments), Discord (verification), GitHub (app downloads and updates), and the ad-network page you choose if you use a free-key checkpoint. Public profile information and anything you publish to Explore is visible to other Luger players. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

Some providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, key and progress data: while you use Luger, and up to 2 years after your last activity.
  • Purchase records: 6 years, for tax and accounting law.
  • Rate-limit counters: minutes to hours.
  • Discord tokens: until you deauthorise Luger or ask us to delete them.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent (for Discord, deauthorise Luger in Discord settings). Ask in a Discord ticket; we reply within one month. If you’re unhappy, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Children

Luger is not for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use Luger with a parent or guardian’s permission. If we learn we hold data about a child under 13, we delete it.

8. Security

Keys are stored scrambled, Discord tokens are encrypted, sessions are digitally signed, all traffic is encrypted, and macros run in a restricted sandbox. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your key private.

9. Changes

We’ll update this page if anything changes and tell you in the app or on Discord about significant changes.
