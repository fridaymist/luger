Cookie Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

lugerhub.fun uses no tracking or advertising cookies. We only use storage that the site needs to work and stay secure, so we don’t ask you to accept anything.

What we use

NameTypePurposeHow long
luger.cookiesLocal storageRemembers that you closed the cookie noticeUntil you clear it
luger.session, luger.stepLocal storageRemembers your progress through free-key checkpointsUntil the checkpoint finishes
__cf_bm, cf_clearanceCookie (Cloudflare)Protects the site from bots and attacks30 minutes to 1 year

Other websites

Pages we link to set their own cookies under their own policies: Stripe on the checkout page, Discord when you verify or join, GitHub when you download, and ad networks if you use a free-key checkpoint.

The Luger app

The desktop app stores your settings, session and macros on your computer. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Controlling storage

You can clear or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may stop checkpoints or security checks from working.
